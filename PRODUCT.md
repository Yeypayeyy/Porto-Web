# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

The portfolio is designed for a broad public audience, including people discovering Muhammad Farrel Al Ghazy through his engineering work, Web3 involvement, campus organizations, events, partnerships, or leadership experience. Visitors should quickly understand who Farrel is, what he can build, and the breadth of his practical experience without needing specialist knowledge.

## Product Purpose

Present Farrel's work and experience as a coherent personal brand, make his capabilities easy to evaluate, and create clear paths to explore projects, understand his background, and start a conversation. Success means visitors leave with a specific, credible impression rather than seeing a generic developer portfolio.

## Brand Personality

Modern, clean, and intentional. The experience should feel technically capable and current, with purposeful GSAP motion that supports storytelling and interaction instead of decorating every element.

## Visual References

- `https://www.amantajati.studio/`: clean, modern, polished smooth-scroll / motion reference (primary atmosphere reference).
- AC Milan away jersey (2006/07): Rossoneri color identity — bone-white canvas, black + red structural blocks, rare Champions gold accent.

The redesign combines clean modern technical credibility and rich scroll choreography from Amanta Jati with a bold Rossoneri visual identity. Use both as atmosphere references, not direct clones. See `DESIGN.md` for the exact palette and motion direction.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated landing-page patterns and interchangeable portfolio templates.
- Old-fashioned visual styling that makes the work feel dated.
- Excessive decorative effects, repetitive card grids, and animation without narrative purpose.

## Design Principles

- Make Farrel recognizable: express a specific personal point of view rather than a generic developer aesthetic.
- Make the range legible: connect engineering, Web3, leadership, and organizational experience into one coherent story.
- Show evidence before claims: prioritize real project artifacts, photography, outcomes, and concrete responsibilities.
- Keep exploration obvious: visitors should understand the main message and next action without learning the interface.
- Use motion as direction: GSAP choreography should clarify hierarchy, pacing, and transitions while leaving content usable without animation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use WCAG 2.2 Level AA as the baseline. Maintain sufficient contrast, visible keyboard focus, semantic structure, descriptive alternatives for meaningful imagery, usable target sizes, and layouts that remain functional under zoom and responsive reflow. Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` with alternatives that preserve all information and functionality.
